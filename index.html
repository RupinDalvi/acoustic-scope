Tips

  • Hold the phone steady, screen toward you, and point the camera toward the area of interest.
  • If stereo isn’t available, gently sway the phone left–right; direction cues improve with slight movement.
  • Short, sharp sounds (snaps, twig breaks) localize better than long booms in outdoor spaces.
  • Wind noise and heavy echo reduce reliability; cup the phone edges to shield mics from wind.
